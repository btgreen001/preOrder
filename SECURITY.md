Reporting a Vulnerability
If you find a security issue, I’d really appreciate you letting me know privately so I can fix it before it becomes a problem for anyone.

How to reach me
Send an email to boardbake@gmail.com with a quick note about what you found. If you can include steps to reproduce it or a small example, that helps a lot.

What happens next
My message might be delayed but I’ll get back to you to confirm I saw your message

I’ll keep you updated while I investigate and work on a fix

Once it’s resolved, I’ll let you know what the outcome was

A small favor
Please avoid posting the issue publicly until I’ve had a chance to look into it. Coordinated disclosure keeps everyone safer.
